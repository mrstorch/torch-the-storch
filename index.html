How to Play 🔥

  1. Pick a number.
  2. Place it on an empty circle.
  3. The computer takes a turn.
  4. Keep playing until only one circle is empty.
  5. That circle becomes the Black Hole and swallows the numbers around it.
  6. The player who loses the fewest points wins!

🔥 Lose fewer points than the computer and YOU TORCH THE STORCH! 🔥
